\subsection{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\myparagraph{Evaluation scope}
Our evaluation is limited to Portrait mode on two commercial devices with different SoCs, using a fixed scene and a 30-capture horizon.
Online duration estimation avoids device- or condition-specific timing tables, but broader validation is needed across devices, camera modes, shot modes, and scenes.
Pacing activation and delay may differ between the saturating ADB loop and intermittent user capture.
We quantify optional-stage retention through stage execution and pacing cost through applied delay.
These metrics do not directly assess image quality or user-perceived responsiveness.

\myparagraph{Cold start}
Without warm-up data, both components lack duration measurements until the first draft sequence completes.
If parallel captures continue arriving during an unusually long first draft sequence, queue pressure can grow before initial duration measurements are available.
We left the implemented warm-up data support disabled because no cold-start timeout was observed in our evaluation and maintaining the dataset would add storage and maintenance costs.
