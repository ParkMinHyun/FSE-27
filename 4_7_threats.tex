\subsection{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

(1) Our evaluation is limited to Portrait mode on two commercial devices with different SoCs, using a fixed scene and a 30-capture horizon.
Although online duration estimation avoids device- or condition-specific timing tables, our results may not generalize to other devices, camera modes, shot modes, and scenes.
(2) Pacing activation and delay may differ between the saturating ADB loop and intermittent user capture.
(3) We measured optional-stage execution and applied pacing delay.
These metrics do not directly assess image quality or user-perceived responsiveness.
(4) Without warm-up data, both components lack duration measurements until the first draft sequence completes.
If parallel captures continue arriving during an unusually long first draft sequence, queue pressure can grow before initial duration measurements are available.
We left the implemented warm-up data support disabled because no cold-start timeout was observed in our evaluation and maintaining the dataset would add storage and maintenance costs.
