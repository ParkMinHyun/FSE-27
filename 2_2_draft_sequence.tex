\subsection{Draft Sequence}
\label{sec:draft-background}

The Samsung Galaxy camera produces the final image through a post-processing pipeline that includes multi-frame composition.
Samsung introduced the draft sequence alongside this pipeline to provide capture results promptly, allowing users to view them before post-processing completes.
The draft sequence comprises the processing stages that produce the draft image, which is replaced by the final image when post-processing completes.
Until this replacement, the draft image cannot be shared or edited.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Extending the draft sequence.}
Visual differences between the draft and final images can make the replacement noticeable to users.
These differences are particularly pronounced for captures with Filter or Watermark enabled.
To apply these effects to the draft image as well, Samsung added the corresponding single-frame stages to the draft sequence.

Advances in post-processing improved final image quality.
The draft image was based on the original image, so the visual gap between draft and final images grew wider.
Figure~\ref{fig:draft-final-gap}(a) and~(c) show an example of this gap in Portrait mode.
Post-processing also required more computation and resources.
To preserve foreground responsiveness, Samsung deferred the start of post-processing in certain shot modes until the camera application entered the background.
This delayed start can prolong the display of the draft image for users who view a photo soon after capture.
This makes reducing the visual gap between draft and final images more important.

To narrow this visual gap, Samsung extended the draft pipeline to support a lightweight version of the multi-frame composition used in post-processing.
Figure~\ref{fig:draft-final-gap}(b) shows the draft image produced with lightweight multi-frame composition, allowing a less noticeable transition to the final image in~(c).
However, the added composition work increased draft processing time and the risk of a Capture Timeout.
Samsung has therefore not deployed lightweight multi-frame composition in the draft sequence on any device model.
