\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Workload control.}
Workload control adapts computation under latency, resource, or thermal constraints~\cite{approxdet-sensys,zilberstein-anytime,loop-perforation-fse,apnet-rtss,branchynet-icpr,mcdnn-mobisys,nestdnn-mobicom,agile3d-mobisys,phoenix-tecs}, including reductions in per-request work under overload~\cite{abdelzaher-content-adaptation,klein-brownout}.
Imprecise computation separates mandatory and optional work, executing optional work when time permits~\cite{shih-imprecise-siam,liu-imprecise-computation}.
Timecard helps servers determine a request's processing budget from elapsed time and predicted downstream delay, leaving the adaptation mechanism service-specific~\cite{timecard-sosp}.
Before each non-preemptible optional draft stage, our admission checks whether the remaining timeout budget covers an empirical upper duration estimate for the entire remaining sequence, including mandatory encoding and saving.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Arrival control.}
Arrival control limits queue growth by regulating request release or admission.
Network senders pace packets to limit bursts and queue buildup~\cite{tcp-pacing-infocom,bbr-queue}, while services throttle arrivals or shed load under overload~\cite{seda-sosp,dagor-socc,breakwater-osdi,aurora-load-shedding}.
Real-time schedulers stretch task periods or skip jobs~\cite{buttazzo-elastic-task,koren-skip-over}.
Preemptive scheduling can also reduce queueing delay by interrupting long-running requests~\cite{shinjuku-nsdi}.
Our pacing regulates the next capture opportunity because accepted captures cannot be rejected and running stages cannot be preempted.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Coordinating workload and arrival control.}
Prior work has also explored the joint control of workload and arrivals.
Feedback-control schedulers adjust request admission and service levels using deadline-miss or utilization feedback~\cite{lu-feedback-control-edf,lu-feedback-control-scheduling}.
Server overload control adjusts admission and service quality against percentile response-time targets and can reject requests~\cite{welsh-overload-control}.
QoS negotiation selects among client-acceptable service levels and uses resource reservation and schedulability analysis to maintain real-time guarantees~\cite{abdelzaher-qos-negotiation}.
Video analytics systems jointly adapt frame sampling rates and processing configurations to balance accuracy against latency and resource limits~\cite{chameleon-sigcomm,videostorm-nsdi,chanakya-neurips}.

Our controller brings such joint control to a single draft worker, coordinating admission and pacing to meet per-capture deadlines without dropping accepted captures or requiring resource reservation.
With duration estimates from completed draft sequences, admission skips reduce the estimated backlog for subsequent pacing decisions, while pacing limits queue growth so that admission can retain more optional work.
