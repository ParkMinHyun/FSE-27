\subsection{Goal}
\label{sec:research-goal}

Our goal is to enable lightweight multi-frame composition in the draft sequence under parallel capture, narrowing the draft--final visual gap while meeting each capture's Capture Timeout deadline.
Because the capture pipeline cannot reject an accepted capture or preempt a running stage, achieving this goal requires controlling both optional draft work and future capture arrivals.
Skipping optional stages can shorten draft processing but cannot recover timeout budget already consumed by queueing.
Delaying delivery of \texttt{captureAvailable} postpones the next capture opportunity while draft processing continues, limiting queue growth and preserving budget for optional processing in later captures.
Both decisions require estimates of draft processing time to determine how much timeout budget to reserve.
Reserving too little time risks a Capture Timeout, whereas reserving too much can needlessly sacrifice draft image quality or capture responsiveness.
