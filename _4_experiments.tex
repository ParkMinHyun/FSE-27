\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

In this section, we evaluate whether coordinated admission and pacing enable MFC in the draft sequence under parallel capture on the production camera stack.
Specifically, we assess deadline safety, optional-stage execution, and pacing delay by addressing the following research questions:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{RQ\arabic*:}, leftmargin=4.8em]
  \item \textbf{End-to-End Effectiveness.}
  How often does our controller execute optional stages without Capture Timeout, and at what pacing cost?

  \item \textbf{Contribution of Admission and Pacing.}
  How do admission and pacing contribute to end-to-end effectiveness, individually and together?

  \item \textbf{Admission Decision Quality.}
  How accurately does admission distinguish feasible from unsafe draft work?

  \item \textbf{Pacing Selectivity.}
  How selectively does pacing intervene under deadline pressure?
\end{enumerate}
